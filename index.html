About

I've worked as a Raw Material Controller, Material Requirement Planner and Country Inventory Deployment Planner at AB InBev, and as a Procurement Officer at Access Bank Plc. In those roles I found I enjoyed the analytical side most: working out why a process fails, what people actually need, and what the numbers say.

I completed an MRes in Computing at the University of Bolton, with a dissertation on machine-learning-based decision support for inventory replenishment. I'm also studying for CIPS Level 4. I'm based in Greater Manchester and looking for Business Analyst roles where supply chain, procurement and data meet.
